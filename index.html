Welcome to XJ Games

Here is a quick guide to shopping on the website.

  • Find products: Browse the categories or search by product name. Use “View All Products” to see the full catalog.
  • Check a product: Open its description for details. Choose the options you want, then add it to your cart. Sign in if asked.
  • Out of stock? Tap “Notify Me” to message XJ Games on WhatsApp about availability.
  • Reduce Price: Open a console’s menu and use “Reduce price” to see the sharing progress and WhatsApp share option.
  • Your cart: Change quantities with +/− or remove an item. Your total updates automatically.
  • Place an order: Tap “Checkout Order” to open a prepared WhatsApp message. Check the products, options, delivery/pickup, and total, then tap Send.
  • Your account: Sign in to manage your account. You can also leave a review, send a recommendation, or contact us from the website.

How to Order

  1. Browse the available products or search the catalog.
  2. Select a product and review its condition and price.
  3. Choose the available product options and quantity.
  4. Add the product to your cart.
  5. Select delivery or pickup during checkout.
  6. If you choose delivery, select or enter your location.
  7. Review the order summary and any confirmed charges.
  8. Open WhatsApp and tap Send to submit your order.
  9. Wait for XJ Games to confirm the order.

Warranty & Returns

Applicable consoles: One week (seven days). If a console develops a fault during this period and the fault was not caused by the customer, XJ Games will offer a repair. Customer-caused faults or damage are not covered. Change-of-mind returns are not accepted. This console policy does not state warranty terms for accessories.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order?

Choose products, options and quantities, add them to your cart, review the checkout summary, then open WhatsApp and tap Send. Wait for XJ Games to confirm.

Where do you deliver and what does it cost?

Delivery is available within The Gambia to the locations and price ranges shown in Delivery & Pickup Information. Exact fees for ambiguous ranges or duplicate locations must be confirmed on WhatsApp.

Can I pick up my order?

Yes. Select Pickup during checkout. Pickup has no delivery fee. The existing website location is Turntable, Brusubi; message XJ Games to confirm arrangements before travelling.

What do product conditions mean?

Condition is shown only when supplied by the admin. If no condition is shown, please ask XJ Games.

Is a product in stock?

Availability can change. Confirm stock on WhatsApp before travelling or paying. Use Notify Me on an out-of-stock product to enquire.

What is the console warranty?

Applicable consoles have a one-week warranty for eligible faults not caused by the customer. The remedy is repair.

Are customer-caused faults or change-of-mind returns covered?

Customer-caused faults or damage are not covered. Change-of-mind returns are not accepted.

How do I contact XJ Games?

Use the WhatsApp contact button on this page or open WhatsApp checkout.

How do I confirm a delivery fee?

Send your selected delivery location with your order on WhatsApp. We do not add an unconfirmed ambiguous fee to the displayed total.
